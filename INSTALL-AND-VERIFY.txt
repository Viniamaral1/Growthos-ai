Install:
1. Copy the frontend folder from this ZIP over the matching frontend folder in the project.
2. From the project root, remove the Next.js cache:
   Remove-Item -Recurse -Force .\frontend\.next -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue
3. Start the frontend normally and hard refresh with Ctrl+Shift+R.

Verify only:
1. Dashboard and other non-chat pages scroll normally.
2. Executive Team shows exactly one scrollbar in the message region.
3. Mouse wheel works while the pointer is over the conversation.
